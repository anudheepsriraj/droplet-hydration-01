3. Physiological Absorption: Micro-Sipping vs. Large Bolus

Comparing intravesical bladder pressure spikes and rapid renal elimination between gulping large cups vs continuous micro-sipping.

🚨 Gulping Large Volumes (250 mL Bolus):

Causes rapid gastric stretch and a sudden drop in blood osmolality. The brain shuts off vasopressin (ADH), resulting in immediate renal water dumping, bladder spasms, and urge incontinence.

✅ Micro-Sipping (30–50 mL Small Sips):

Matches small intestine mucosal absorption rates. Osmolality remains steady, cellular hydration is maximized, and bladder pressure stays well below contraction thresholds.
